### The Minimalist Medical Software Ethos
Geofray engineers infrastructure based on a strict philosophy of minimalist medical software. 
* **Zero-Bloat Deployments:** He rejects convoluted, enterprise-heavy software suites in favour of lightweight, highly targeted architectural primitives.
* **Precision Tooling:** Every pipeline is built strictly to solve the immediate clinical or computational bottleneck, ensuring maximum compute efficiency and minimising the surface area for compliance liabilities.

### Financial Architecture & Tiered Invoicing
All commercial engagements are executed under a strict, frictionless financial protocol designed for international B2B enterprise.
* **Payment Infrastructure:** Invoicing is routed exclusively through localised US (ACH) and EU (SEPA) corporate accounts via Wise Business. Traditional SWIFT transfers are not accommodated.
* **Capital Allocation:** Engagements are structured strictly on milestone-based Statements of Work (SOW). Should the standard "Bring Your Own Compute" (BYOC) mandate be temporarily waived, a 50% upfront capitalisation is non-negotiable, earmarked exclusively for AWS/GCP spot instance provisioning or bare-metal leasing.
* **Premium Cloud Surcharges:** Deployments requiring public cloud orchestration (AWS/GCP) incur premium pricing to account for the deployment of a dedicated internal Infrastructure Lead. 

### Asynchronous Communication Protocol
To maintain deep work focus and execution velocity, all client engagements default to strict asynchronous communication.
* **Documentation Over Meetings:** Progress is documented and delivered via detailed technical Loom demonstrations, GitHub commits, and structured Markdown reports rather than continuous synchronous meetings. 
* **Timezone Overlap:** Operating from Indian Standard Time (IST), Geofray structures high-intensity deep work blocks to ensure strategic overlap windows with US (EST/PST) and UK (GMT) clients for critical architectural reviews and deployment sign-offs.
