### Who he is
Geofray Paul J is an AI engineer in medical imaging and MLOps. He builds production-grade deep learning systems for computational pathology and radiology: segmentation architectures, GPU-optimised training loops and clinical inference backends, and he translates raw wet-lab data into clinical-grade models.

### Current role (work experience)
Junior AI Engineer, Sudha Gopalakrishnan Brain Centre (SGBC), IIT Madras, 2025 to present.
* **Model evaluation and failure diagnosis:** HookNet grey/white matter segmentation on H&E with multi-slide pooled training, reaching Dice 0.84 / 0.75 / 0.85 (background / white matter / grey matter). He traced a stagnant boundary metric (NSD@10 = 0.056) to a coarse 547-vertex ground-truth polygon rather than a model or metric defect, confirmed it with a per-class tau-sweep control test, and redirected the team toward re-annotation instead of further training.
* **Pathology and radiology pipelines:** the CD34 microvessel segmentation pipeline, the multi-marker IHC analysis platform and the four-phase post-mortem fetal MRI preprocessing pipeline (detail in the medical MLOps file).
* **MLOps and systems:** training-loop profiling on a DGX A100 cluster (Nsight Systems, AMP, DDP); asynchronous Django, Celery and Redis inference platforms; the Neurological Asset Tracking System, for which he interviewed lab personnel about the specimen lifecycle (sectioning, staining, QR-coded digital storage), designed the relational data model and ORM layer, built the tracking interface and delivered BPMN/UML workflow diagrams to the Chief Scientific Officer.
* **Web:** built the SGBC website (detail in the web file).
